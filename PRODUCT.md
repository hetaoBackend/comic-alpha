# ComicPerfect

Local comic creation console for a creator working with a private ComicBrain Wiki.
Two paths: continue a series using its principles and story memory, or create a standalone comic from an idea. Creators edit story drafts before generating storyboards and images. Series and completed comics remain browsable with their history.

The supplied comicperfect-ux.html is the visual and interaction authority. Preserve Codex, Gemini and OpenAI-compatible generation, style references, covers, exports and social copy. Private Wiki content is available only through localhost APIs; the supported static server must never expose the repository. Production drafts and images are not canon. Clicking 保存本话到系列 explicitly authorizes one direct save with continuity checks, evidence and conflict detection. Final authored panels and covers are serialized by code; AI extracts a compact summary and affected state changes, generates or updates a concise retrospective when needed, and evaluates edits against the AI original. No second confirmation. Local choices stay local; quality lessons become scoped candidates. Explicit future guidance can update series creative requirements after professional judgment. Never promote one local edit into a global rule. Preserve manually edited retrospectives and offer independent adjustment.

User-facing language treats the workspace as one product. Use 创作原则 and 创作记忆; do not expose the ComicBrain name, internal Wiki/production storage terms, or split-product responsibility taglines in navigation, help, settings, status or confirmation copy. Internal integrations and stored identifiers remain compatible.

The contextual 创作助手 uses the configured text provider, including Codex, for multi-turn discussion and scoped writing edits. It is available globally and beside story, page, cover and creative-document content. Always name the selected target and whether changes affect this draft, one series or all series. Discussion does not mutate content. Concrete edits show before/after previews before adoption; draft edits preserve generated image history and support conflict-aware undo. Document adoption preserves identity and status, records evidence, and validates a staged workspace before updating current files. Never infer a permanent preference from one local change. History and unsent instructions are scoped to the workspace and target. Cover, publishing and export actions remain discoverable in the output stage.

创作空间 is a visible sidebar destination showing the current folder, recent spaces, open-existing and create-empty actions. Desktop folder selection is native and trusted; switching waits for pending saves, then relaunches into the target. Startup-configured roots are explicitly locked. Creating a space copies reusable behavior and an empty Wiki only, never another creator's canon, assets or production. Browser-only use explains that the running service selects its workspace. ComicPerfect's product mark combines comic panels with a speech bubble; interface, favicon and macOS icon share the asset under assets/brand.
